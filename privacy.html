Privacy Policy

Specter, by Higher Bar, LLC. Last updated November 14, 2026.

Specter is a viewer for your own Turing Vision security cameras. We, Higher Bar, LLC, do not collect, store, sell or share any information about you. Specter has no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your camera account

When you sign in, Specter sends your email and password directly to Turing Vision to start a session, then talks to Turing Vision on your behalf to show your cameras, recordings and detections. That data travels between your device and Turing Vision only. Turing Vision’s own privacy policy covers what it keeps. Specter saves only a session token, in your device’s Keychain, so you stay signed in. If you turn on Face ID or Touch ID sign-in, your password is kept in the Keychain behind it.

Other services you choose to connect

If you connect an alarm system or a time clock in the app, Specter talks to that service directly with the sign-in you give it, in the same way. Nothing passes through us.

On your device and in your iCloud

  • Recordings, clips and snapshots you save stay on your device (and in your photo library if you save them there).
  • Face matching runs on your device. The face data it uses stays on your device and in your own iCloud account.
  • Your settings, saved views and names you give to cars and people sync between your devices through your own iCloud account. We can’t see them.

Permissions

The microphone is used only while you hold Talk, to speak through a camera’s speaker. Photo library access is used only to save the snapshots and clips you choose. Face ID or Touch ID is used only to sign you in.

Children

Specter is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: support@higherbarapps.com.
