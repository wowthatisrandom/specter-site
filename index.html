Specter

A fast viewer for Turing Vision security cameras on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Made by Higher Bar, LLC.

Get help

Email support@higherbarapps.com. We usually answer within one business day.

What you need

Specter shows the cameras in your own Turing Vision account. Sign in with the same email and password you use for Turing Vision. Specter has no accounts of its own.

Common questions

A camera says “Connecting…” and never plays. Check that the camera is online in Turing Vision. On a weak connection, turn off HD previews in Settings.

Playback shows no recordings. Recordings come from your Turing box. Pick another day, or check that the box is online.

Where are my clips? Clips you save stay on your device: in the Files app on iPhone and iPad, and in your Movies folder on Mac.

How do I sign out? Open Settings in the app and choose Sign Out.
